The missing layer

The internet made information easy to publish. It never made it easy to verify.

AI makes that urgent: agents act at scale while their claims and authority stay locked inside the platforms that run them. CIRIS adds the missing layer — evidence that travels with the claim, and governance that travels with the relationship.

THE IDEA UNDER EVERYTHING

CIRIS separates intelligence from authority, and makes authority accountable through evidence.

The web with its receipts

Every act is a signed row. Anyone can check it. Its author can take it back.

A message, a vote, a consent, a moderation decision, an agent’s action — five kinds of row, and no sixth.
